Airbnb Profit Calculator: Run the Numbers Before You Buy

Updated June 2026 · 6 min read

Every Airbnb deal looks profitable in the listing photos. An Airbnb profit calculator exists to answer the only question that matters before you wire a deposit: after every real cost, does this property actually make money? Here's how to use one properly — including the five inputs that drive the answer and the traps that quietly inflate results.

The 5 inputs that decide the answer

  1. Purchase price + cash in — price, down payment, closing costs, furnishing. Your true investment, not just the sticker.
  2. ADR (average daily rate) — from real comparable listings, not the best week of the year.
  3. Occupancy — a normal year. In many decent markets that's 55–70%, not the 90% a hot month suggests.
  4. Operating costs — cleaning, platform fees, management, STR insurance, utilities, software, supplies, property tax, and a maintenance reserve. For many STRs this lands around 35–50% of revenue.
  5. Financing — loan amount, rate, term. The mortgage is usually the biggest single line.

A worked example (3 minutes, honest numbers)

Say you're looking at a $400,000 property: 20% down plus closing and furnishing puts roughly $107,000 of cash in. Comps support an ADR of $220 at 62% normal-year occupancy.

That's a ~1.8% cash-on-cash return and a DSCR of about 1.07 — the photos said yes, the calculator says not at this price. Maybe the deal works at $360,000, or with an ADR the comps can actually defend. That negotiation insight is the entire point of running the numbers first.

A calculator doesn't kill deals. It kills prices — and tells you the one the deal works at.

The traps that inflate calculator results

  • Peak-season ADR for all 365 days. Use the year-round average from comps.
  • Gross revenue treated as profit. Revenue estimators stop before costs; profit lives after them.
  • Skipping management. Even self-managers should model 10–25% — your weekends have a price.
  • No maintenance reserve. One HVAC failure erases a quarter of "profit" you never really had.
  • Ignoring break-even occupancy. If you need 75% just to cover costs, one slow season puts you underwater.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are Airbnb profit calculators?

As accurate as the inputs. Honest ADR, normal-year occupancy and the full cost stack get you close enough to screen with confidence.

What's a good profit margin for an Airbnb?

Positive cash flow after every cost including the mortgage, roughly 8%+ cash-on-cash, and a DSCR above ~1.25 are common bars.

Is Airbnb still profitable in 2026?

Property by property. Supply, regulation and rates vary too much for a headline answer — underwrite the specific deal.
